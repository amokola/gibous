GIBOUS DUEL ARENA • PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect public Telegram identifier information (Telegram ID, first name, username) and connected TON public wallet addresses for game session management, escrow settlements, and leaderboard rankings.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We never collect, request, or store private keys, seed phrases, or external passwords. All wallet transactions require explicit manual authorization in your non-custodial wallet via TON Connect.

3. How We Use Data

Your public identity is used exclusively to facilitate peer-to-peer matchmaking, calculate match payouts, display leaderboard positions, and maintain account state.

4. Third-Party Services

Transactions interact directly with the public TON blockchain network and RPC providers (Toncenter). On-chain transaction records are public by design.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us via Telegram at @gibous_support.
